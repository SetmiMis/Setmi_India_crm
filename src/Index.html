How it Works

Ek enquiry system mein aane se close hone tak kaise chalti hai — naye team member ke liye.
1
Enquiry aati hai

Teen tareeke: New Enquiry tab se staff khud daalta hai, public Field Link se koi bhi bhejta hai, ya Bulk Import se ek saath CSV/Excel upload hoti hai.

2
Review — Pending Approvals

Teeno tareeke pehle Pending Approvals mein jaate hain, kyunki inmein data galat/adhura ho sakta hai — ek baar dekh kar Approve karna padta hai. Approve hote hi enquiry Available Leads mein chali jaati hai.

3
Claim — jaise order accept karna

Available Leads mein sabko unclaimed leads dikhte hain. Koi bhi rep lead ko "Call & Claim" dabata hai — ek hi popup mein call bhi ho jaati hai aur Priority set karke Claim bhi. Lead automatically usi rep ke naam ho jaata hai — kisi ko manually assign nahi karna padta.

4
Pipeline mein follow-up

Claim hone ke baad lead Pipeline mein dikhta hai. Priority ke hisaab se agli follow-up date apne aap ban jaati hai (Hot jaldi, Cold der se). Follow-ups tab par roz ke overdue/due-today leads dikhte hain.

5
Won / Lost / Dispatched

Deal final hone par Stage badal do — Won, Dispatched, Closed (sab "converted" count hote hain), ya Lost.

Yaad rakhein
  • Lead khud se kisi ko assign nahi hota — jo call karke claim karega, wahi owner banega.
  • Priority call ke baad hi set hoti hai — isi liye claim step mein hi priority + call log ek saath hai.
  • Pending Approvals sirf un leads ke liye hai jinka data pehle verify karna zaroori hai (manual entry / Field Link / Bulk Import).
  • Rep sirf apni khud ki leads dekh/edit kar sakta hai (Pipeline, Follow-ups) — Reports, Team Performance aur Users sirf Admin ko dikhte hain.
